# UISS Community

The shared language for the people, communities, and participation experiences represented by the UISS website.

## Clubs

**Club**:
An ongoing UISS student community organized around a technical field, with members, leaders, activities, events, and projects. The six peer clubs are Networking, Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, UI/UX & Graphic Design, Data Science, and Blockchain.
_Avoid_: Course, learning track, content category

**Published Club Profile**:
The verified public representation of a club that meets the minimum content standard for appearing on the UISS website.
_Avoid_: Active club

**Archived Club**:
A club that no longer operates or accepts members but may retain an explicitly approved, read-only public profile as part of UISS history.
_Avoid_: Active club, unpublished club

**Club Membership**:
A student’s active participation in a club; one student may hold memberships in several clubs.
_Avoid_: Club interest, learning-track enrollment

**Primary Club**:
The one club a student identifies as their main affiliation while remaining free to participate in other clubs.
_Avoid_: Exclusive club, only club

**Student Member**:
A current University of Dar es Salaam student whose single UISS membership application has been approved. Alumni and external professionals are not student members, even when they participate as mentors or partners.
_Avoid_: Website user, applicant, mentor

**Additional Club**:
One of up to two clubs a student selects alongside their primary club when applying for UISS membership.
_Avoid_: Secondary membership, club interest

**Membership Application**:
A current student’s single request to join UISS with one primary club and up to two additional clubs; it remains distinct from approved club membership.
_Avoid_: Registration, club interest form

**Annual Confirmation**:
The lightweight yearly check through which a continuing student confirms their eligibility, contact information, and club choices without submitting a new membership application.
_Avoid_: Reapplication, semester registration

**Lapsed Membership**:
A previously approved student membership that is no longer active because annual confirmation was not completed within the renewal period; it may be reactivated after eligibility is reconfirmed.
_Avoid_: Deleted member, rejected member

**Membership Change Request**:
An email-verified, accountless request from a student to change their primary club, add or leave clubs, withdraw an application, or end UISS membership.
_Avoid_: New application, unverified message

## Leadership

**General Leadership**:
The eleven-person team responsible for UISS as a whole rather than for one technical club.
_Avoid_: Club leadership, all leaders

**Club Lead**:
A person responsible for leading one specific club. A Club Lead may simultaneously belong to General Leadership, but the roles are independent and most Club Leads are outside the eleven-person team.
_Avoid_: General Leader, automatic executive member

**Deputy Club Lead**:
An optional appointment supporting a Club Lead for one specific club without replacing the Club Lead’s accountability.
_Avoid_: Co-owner, automatic successor

**Leadership Appointment**:
A time-bounded assignment connecting one person to a General Leadership or Club Leadership role for a defined term; one person may hold several appointments.
_Avoid_: Duplicate leader profile, permanent title

**Club Leadership**:
The current Club Lead, optional Deputy Club Lead, and any formally appointed committee roles responsible for one club.
_Avoid_: General Leadership, club members

**Clubs Coordinator**:
The designated General Leadership appointment accountable for coordinating club leads and reviewing club-owned public content; its displayed title follows the UISS constitution.
_Avoid_: Website Administrator, Club Lead

**Club Editor**:
A Club Lead, Deputy Club Lead, or formally delegated committee member authorized to prepare content only for their assigned club and submit it for review.
_Avoid_: Website Administrator, publisher

**Website Administrator**:
A system-level custodian responsible for access, platform operations, and emergency content controls rather than routine ownership of club information.
_Avoid_: Club Editor, Clubs Coordinator

## Internal Operations

**UISS Operations Platform**:
The private workspace through which authorized UISS leaders operate membership, clubs, projects, KPIs, events, publishing, leadership access, and audit processes as separate modules.
_Avoid_: Public website, one-page dashboard

**Project Manager**:
One of the two General Leadership appointments responsible for coordinating UISS projects and operational review; the appointment does not itself grant unrestricted technical access.
_Avoid_: Superadmin, Website Administrator

**Platform Role**:
A revocable collection of system permissions assigned independently from a person’s organizational appointment and limited by module and scope.
_Avoid_: Leadership title, permanent access

**Operations Owner**:
The day-to-day platform role held by an authorized Project Manager for operational oversight, subject to audit and two-person approval for high-risk actions.
_Avoid_: Technical superadmin, unrestricted account

**Technical Custodian**:
A tightly controlled, non-routine platform role used for security, recovery, and system-level administration rather than ordinary UISS operations.
_Avoid_: Project Manager, Operations Owner

**Member Contributor**:
An approved student member with private, restricted access to their own profile, assigned project work, milestones, and evidence rather than administrative member records.
_Avoid_: Club Manager, administrator

**Club Manager**:
A club-scoped Platform Role held by an approved Club Lead or deputy for operating their assigned club without society-wide access.
_Avoid_: Operations Owner, unrestricted administrator

**Reviewer/Publisher**:
A scoped Platform Role that may review assigned publication requests and publish approved public content but cannot approve its holder’s own submissions.
_Avoid_: Club Editor, Operations Owner

**Read-only Auditor**:
A Platform Role permitted to inspect authorized operational records and audit history without changing either.
_Avoid_: Reviewer, administrator

**Access Assignment**:
A time-bounded grant connecting one person to one Platform Role with an explicit module or record scope, source, start, expiry, and revocation state.
_Avoid_: Organizational appointment, permanent account type

**Temporary Delegation**:
A named, narrowly scoped Access Assignment created for short-term operational cover with a recorded reason and automatic expiry.
_Avoid_: Shared account, password sharing

**High-risk Action**:
An operation whose security, privacy, governance, or organizational impact requires one authorized person to propose it and a different authorized person to approve it before execution.
_Avoid_: Routine edit, ordinary reversible action

**Dual Approval**:
The conflict-free propose–approve–execute control in which two independently authorized people confirm an unchanged High-risk Action before it takes effect.
_Avoid_: Self-approval, two shared accounts

**Audit Entry**:
An append-only record explaining a significant action, access event, decision, outcome, responsible identity, scope, timing, and related authorization without storing credentials or unnecessary sensitive content.
_Avoid_: Editable activity note, raw secret log

**Audit Correction**:
A linked counter-entry that explains and corrects an inaccurate Audit Entry while preserving the original history.
_Avoid_: Audit edit, silent deletion

**Emergency Access Session**:
A disabled-by-default, narrowly scoped, dual-approved Technical Custodian session protected by recent MFA, automatic expiry, immediate notification, complete audit, and subsequent review.
_Avoid_: Permanent administrator access, shared recovery account

**Protective Action**:
An immediate, audited response that reduces access or pauses exposure during suspected harm without granting power, erasing records, approving work, or altering evidence.
_Avoid_: Policy override, emergency approval

**Incident Record**:
The governed account of a suspected or confirmed operational or security incident, including severity, scope, owner, timeline, containment, affected records or people, communications, recovery, and review.
_Avoid_: Informal message thread, Audit Entry

## Events and Publication

**Event Owner**:
The one named authorized person accountable for the accuracy, readiness, updates, and follow-through of a Club, Society, or Collaborative Event even when several groups participate.
_Avoid_: Event attendee, all collaborating organizations

**Club Event**:
An event owned by one UISS club and operated through that club's scoped publication workflow.
_Avoid_: Society Event, club meeting record

**Society Event**:
An event owned by UISS as a whole rather than by one technical club.
_Avoid_: Club Event, Collaborative Event

**Collaborative Event**:
An event with one accountable UISS owner and named participating clubs or external partners.
_Avoid_: Shared ownership, ownerless partnership event

**Public Content Revision**:
One immutable version of proposed public fields, media, destinations, and publication timing to which a review decision is bound.
_Avoid_: Editable approved draft, live record

**Event Lifecycle**:
The governed editorial progression Draft, Submitted, Changes Requested, Approved, Scheduled, Published, Completed, Cancelled, or Archived for one event, distinct from its calculated calendar timing.
_Avoid_: Upcoming label, publication-delivery result

**Public Timing Label**:
The automatically calculated Upcoming, Happening Now, or Past description derived from an event's approved dates.
_Avoid_: Event Lifecycle, reviewer decision

**Publication Delivery State**:
The Pending, Published, Failed, Out of Sync, or Unpublished result describing whether the public website has confirmed delivery of one exact approved revision.
_Avoid_: Event Lifecycle, scheduled intention

## Projects and Progress

**Individual Member Project**:
A project accountable to one student member that may include collaborators and may connect to an externally hosted repository.
_Avoid_: Club Project, personal repository

**Club Project**:
A project sponsored by and accountable to one UISS club, delivered by named contributors, and hosted in the UISS GitHub organization.
_Avoid_: Individual Member Project, Society Project

**Society Project**:
A cross-club or institution-wide project sponsored by UISS rather than by one club and hosted in the UISS GitHub organization.
_Avoid_: Club Project, unrelated repository

**Project Evidence**:
A verifiable artifact used during human review to judge a declared goal or milestone, including but not limited to GitHub activity.
_Avoid_: Commit count, undocumented score

**UISS-Verified Project**:
A project whose ownership, evidence, milestones, and quality have received an explicit human verification decision; repository connection alone does not confer this status.
_Avoid_: Connected repository, active repository

**KPI**:
An explainable, time-bound measure tied to a declared goal, target, evidence, and review process for a project, member, or club.
_Avoid_: Universal score, leaderboard rank

**KPI Result**:
A contextual review outcome of Not Started, On Track, At Risk, Met, Not Met, or Not Applicable against one agreed KPI; it is not a disciplinary decision or universal score.
_Avoid_: Member rank, automatic penalty

**Recovery Plan**:
A documented, time-bound agreement describing context, blockers, support, adjusted actions, and follow-up after a KPI becomes At Risk or Not Met.
_Avoid_: Automatic punishment, removal decision

**KPI Review**:
A documented human assessment of one KPI's target, evidence, context, and result by an authorized reviewer who has no conflict of interest.
_Avoid_: Automated score, disciplinary hearing

**KPI Template**:
A versioned, effective-dated reusable definition for a class of project, member, or club KPIs that has completed the required organizational approvals.
_Avoid_: Retroactive target, universal scorecard

**KPI Appeal**:
A time-bounded request to reconsider a KPI Result using additional evidence and an independent qualified reviewer while preserving the original decision and final outcome.
_Avoid_: Silent result edit, informal complaint

**Project Owner**:
The one named member or appointed leader accountable for coordinating a project’s delivery, contributors, milestones, evidence, and status.
_Avoid_: Sponsor, Project Manager Reviewer

**Project Sponsor**:
The Club Lead or General Leader who provides organizational backing, confirms purpose and scope, and resolves escalations without owning day-to-day delivery.
_Avoid_: Project Owner, reviewer

**Project Contributor**:
A named participant assigned to deliver specific project work and evidence without carrying overall project accountability.
_Avoid_: Project Owner, external observer

**Project Manager Reviewer**:
An authorized Project Manager who independently evaluates feasibility, progress, evidence, and completion without becoming the project’s delivery owner or approving their own work.
_Avoid_: Project Owner, Project Sponsor

**Project Milestone**:
An outcome checkpoint with a named owner, target date, acceptance criteria, and required Project Evidence.
_Avoid_: Completion percentage, unverified task list

**Project Health**:
A current delivery signal of On Track, At Risk, or Blocked that remains separate from the project’s lifecycle state.
_Avoid_: Project status, performance score

**Project Baseline**:
The approved version of a project’s problem, outcome, scope, dates, ownership, milestones, evidence plan, and review cadence against which material changes are reviewed.
_Avoid_: Latest draft, overwritten plan

**Delivery Completion**:
The Project Sponsor’s acceptance that all required milestones meet their criteria; it does not itself confer UISS verification or public publication.
_Avoid_: UISS verification, publication

**Repository Connection**:
A reviewed link between one UISS project and one GitHub repository whose operational state is Pending, Connected, Stale, Verification Lost, or Disconnected.
_Avoid_: Project, UISS verification

**Primary Repository**:
The one connected repository designated as the main code or technical artifact source for a project; a project may also have purpose-defined supporting repositories.
_Avoid_: Project record, only permissible evidence

**Organization Ownership**:
The repository classification UISS-owned, Externally Hosted, or Unverified, determined independently from repository connectivity and project quality.
_Avoid_: Repository Connection, UISS verification

**GitHub Identity Link**:
A member-authorized association between their UISS profile and a GitHub account verified and retained by GitHub's durable account ID.
_Avoid_: Commit-email match, inferred identity

**Project Verification State**:
The human-governed state Not Submitted, Under Review, Changes Requested, Verified, or Verification Withdrawn describing whether a project's outcomes and evidence satisfy the UISS verification standard.
_Avoid_: Repository Connection, Organization Ownership
